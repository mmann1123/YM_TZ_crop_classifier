## Key Findings

(All are unverified secondary assertions, reproduced only to convey the document's content.)

- OSM demonstrates that open-source principles — permissionless contribution, copyleft licensing, distributed governance — work for geographic *data* as they do for software.
- The ODbL's produced-work/derivative-database split makes OSM commercially usable while keeping the underlying database a protected commons.
- Quality is maintained post-hoc via community vigilance (OSMCha, JOSM validator, the Data Working Group) rather than pre-publication gatekeeping, with claimed low vandalism rates.
- Major corporations (Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, TomTom, Apple, Lyft, Uber, Grab) both consume OSM and contribute back, creating a "virtuous cycle"; Overture conflates 200+ sources into a standardized enterprise product while preserving ODbL for OSM-derived themes.
- OSM underpins humanitarian response (HOT, Missing Maps) and is "critical shared infrastructure" for navigation, transit, and disaster relief.
